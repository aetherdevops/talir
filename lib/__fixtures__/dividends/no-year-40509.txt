Article 4 The first day of trading without the right to dividend shall be 03.12.2015. Article 5 The payment of the dividend shall be effectuated in the period between 18.12.2015 and 29.12.2015. Article 6 The Company shall inform the shareholders about the details related to the dividend payment, with a respective announcement after the adoption of the Resolution on payment of dividend and determination of the dividend calendar by the Shareholders’ Assembly. 1 Article 7 This Resolution shall enter into force on the day of its adoption.
